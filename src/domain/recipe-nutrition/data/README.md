# USDA nutrient profiles

`sr-legacy.json` contains 102 selected public-domain food/portion records from the
USDA FoodData Central **SR Legacy, April 2018** JSON download. Nutrients are per
100 g; energy is kcal, sodium mg, and other included nutrients g. Original FDC
IDs, descriptions and published portion gram weights are retained.

Source: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/
Archive: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_json_2018-04.zip

Aliases are reviewed application mappings, not USDA names. Unspecified rice and
pasta assume dry/raw unenriched food; unspecified meats assume the stated raw
cut. Beans require an explicit canned, cooked or dried form. Egg counts assume
large eggs. These assumptions are shown in the recipe's nutrition sources.
Unsupported foods, portions and unmeasured extras remain missing. No USDA
mass/volume conversion is used for inventory deductions or shopping.
